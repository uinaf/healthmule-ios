A short route out

From Health to files.
Without the detour.

Your iPhone reads the metrics you enable, prepares daily summaries, and uploads them directly to Google Drive. Your health data never passes through a HealthMule server.

01 / SELECT

Choose what comes along.

Enable only the supported metrics you want to export, then grant read access in Apple Health. HealthMule never requests permission to write health data.

02 / CONNECT

Give it a small corner of Drive.

Sign in with Google. HealthMule uses per-file access to create and manage its exports, without permission to browse your whole Drive.

03 / EXPORT

Keep a daily record.

Daily JSON files and a manifest live in Apple Health Sync. Updated days are reconciled there. Use the files with the tools you choose.

A deliberate selection

Useful daily summaries.
Not your entire medical history.

HealthMule supports these Apple Health types. You choose which to enable. Missing or unreadable measurements stay empty in the export; they are never turned into a made-up zero.

  • Body mass
  • Steps
  • Active energy
  • Resting energy
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart-rate variability
  • VO₂ max
  • Sleep
  • Workouts

Fewer places for your data to go

Your phone does the work.
Your Drive keeps the copy.

No HealthMule account. No developer database of health records. No advertising or tracking SDKs.

Read the privacy policy
  • Protected on your iPhone.Local export staging uses file protection and is excluded from device backups. Google sign-in credentials stay in Keychain.
  • A lightweight Watch companion.The Watch shows sync status and can request a sync when your iPhone is reachable. It does not receive health values.
  • You stay in control.Disconnect Google at any time. Existing exports remain in your Drive until you delete them yourself.

A small practical note

Sync follows iOS’s schedule.

Background activity is controlled by iOS. Open the app to review status or start a sync.
