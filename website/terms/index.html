A few practical terms

Terms of use

Effective October 18, 2026

HealthMule is a tool for exporting selected Apple Health data to your own Google Drive. You control the permissions, account, and exported files.

The app

HealthMule is provided by undefined is not a function LLC (uinaf). It reads supported Apple Health types with your permission and prepares daily JSON exports. An iPhone performs the sync; the Apple Watch companion shows status and can request a sync when the phone is reachable.

Your account and files

Use a Google account you are authorized to access. You are responsible for the account’s security, available Drive storage, and any sharing or reuse of your exported files. HealthMule does not claim ownership of your health data or exports.

Apple and Google services are subject to their own terms. The privacy policy explains HealthMule’s use of HealthKit, Google sign-in, local storage, and Drive.

Sync and data limits

Exports depend on the information Apple Health makes available, your permissions, connectivity, and Google Drive availability. Missing or unreadable values can be empty. iOS decides when background execution can happen, so HealthMule cannot guarantee a fixed sync schedule or uninterrupted delivery.

Keep independent copies of files you need. Disconnecting HealthMule or resetting its local sync state does not remove files already stored in your Drive. Exported summaries are for your own information and tools; HealthMule is not a medical diagnosis or treatment service.
